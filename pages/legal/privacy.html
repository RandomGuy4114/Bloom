1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, Bloom may collect:

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Password (securely managed through our authentication provider)
  • Profile information you choose to provide, such as your bio or profile picture

2. Community Information

Bloom stores information necessary to operate communities, including:

  • Communities you create
  • Communities you join
  • Posts and comments you publish
  • Events and other content you choose to share

3. Location Information

Bloom uses location information to support location-based features such as discovering or joining nearby communities.

Bloom does not continuously track your live location.

Location is only accessed when required for features that use it and only with your permission.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your profile
  • Show content from communities you join
  • Operate location-based community features
  • Improve Bloom and its features
  • Protect the platform against abuse and spam

5. Information We Share

Bloom does not sell your personal information.

Some profile information, posts, comments, and community activity may be visible to other users depending on your privacy settings and the communities you participate in.

Bloom may disclose information if required by applicable law.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

Bloom may use cookies or browser storage to:

  • Keep you signed in
  • Remember your preferences
  • Improve your experience

These technologies are not used to sell your personal information.

7. Data Security

Bloom takes reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account.

When an account is deleted, Bloom will remove or anonymize personal information where reasonably possible, subject to legal obligations and technical limitations.

Some public content may remain if necessary to preserve community discussions.

9. Children's Privacy

Bloom is not intended for individuals below the minimum age required by applicable law to use online services without appropriate permission.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest version.

Your continued use of Bloom after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Bloom project through its official GitHub repository or other official contact channels.

Thank you for helping us build a safe and community-focused platform.
