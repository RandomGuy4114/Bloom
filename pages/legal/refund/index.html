1. Overview

Bloom offers optional paid subscriptions, such as Bloom Supporter, to help fund the continued development of the platform.

Core features of Bloom remain available without purchasing a subscription.

2. Bloom Supporter Subscription

When you purchase a Bloom Supporter subscription, you receive access to the supporter benefits available at the time of your subscription.

Supporter benefits may change over time as Bloom continues to develop.

3. Refund Eligibility

If you believe you were charged in error, please contact Bloom as soon as possible.

Refund requests are reviewed individually and may be granted in circumstances such as:

  • Duplicate payments.
  • Accidental purchases.
  • Technical issues that prevented access to supporter benefits.
  • Other situations determined appropriate by the Bloom team.

Refunds are not guaranteed and are issued at Bloom's discretion unless required by applicable law.

4. Subscription Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time through the payment provider used for your purchase.

Unless otherwise stated by your payment provider or required by law, cancellation stops future recurring charges but does not automatically refund previous payments.

Your supporter benefits will generally remain active until the end of your current billing period.

5. Exceptions

Refunds may be denied if:

  • The subscription was actively used for a significant portion of the billing period.
  • The request is fraudulent or abusive.
  • The payment provider determines the transaction is valid and no refund is required.

6. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback with your payment provider, Bloom reserves the right to suspend or terminate your supporter benefits while the dispute is being investigated.

We encourage users to contact Bloom first so we can resolve payment issues quickly.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Refund Policy may be updated from time to time.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest version.

Your continued purchase or use of Bloom Supporter after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Refund Policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about refunds or your subscription, please contact the Bloom project through its official GitHub repository or other official contact channels.

Thank you for supporting the continued development of Bloom.
